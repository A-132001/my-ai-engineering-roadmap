Rules for Your Journey

These are intentionally personalized to your target.

01 — Don't become a Data Scientist

Statistics, Pandas and classical ML are foundations. Your destination is AI systems, not analytics.

02 — Use your engineering advantage

You already understand software engineering. Apply it to AI instead of restarting from zero.

03 — Build while learning

Every major concept should end in a small working artifact. Avoid six months of passive courses.

04 — Learn RAG deeply

Don't just use a framework. Understand parsing, chunking, embeddings, retrieval, reranking and evaluation.

05 — Don't framework-hop

One solid stack is better than knowing the names of ten agent frameworks.

06 — Production matters

Latency, cost, security, observability, evaluation and deployment are part of AI engineering—not extras.
